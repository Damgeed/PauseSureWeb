# PauseSure paid-media launch playbook

Updated: August 24, 2026

## Decision

Use paid media to amplify proven product demand—not to discover whether the product retains users. The first commercial target is **cost per activated, retained user**, not the cheapest install.

## Creative system

### Master A — pressure interruption (15 seconds)

| Time | Visual | On-screen message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Pressure language and PauseSure mark | “Scams manufacture pressure.” |
| 2–5s | Input chooser | “One calm place to inspect it.” |
| 5–8s | Explainable high-risk result | “See why it looks risky.” |
| 8–12s | Pause intervention and recovery | “Pause. Verify. Recover.” |
| 12–15s | Mark, iPhone status, URL | “Pause before you act.” |

Deliverables:

- `PauseSure-Ad-Vertical-15s-1080x1920.mp4` — Reels, Stories, Shorts, vertical mobile inventory;
- `PauseSure-Ad-Landscape-15s-1920x1080.mp4` — YouTube and landscape video inventory;
- matching poster frames for upload and review;
- a web-optimized 720p silent version embedded on pausesure.com.

The master must remain understandable with audio muted. The current audio bed is original, restrained sound design; commission a human voiceover only after the winning hook is known.

### Test B — family second opinion (6–10 seconds)

Hook: “When a message feels off, you don't have to decide alone.”

Show Trusted Circle selection, selective-sharing controls, and “Help without taking over.” End on the “Help someone I trust” custom product page.

### Test C — recovery (6–10 seconds)

Hook: “Money moved? The next minutes matter.”

Show bank contact, account containment, evidence preservation, and secure export. Never imply fund recovery, law-enforcement affiliation, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Platform plan

### Apple Ads — first paid channel

Why: high-intent App Store discovery and the shortest path from ad to install. Apple says 70% of App Store visitors use search and almost 65% of downloads happen directly after a search. [Apple Ads overview](https://ads.apple.com/app-store)

Structure:

1. Brand exact match.
2. Category exact match: scam protection, scam checker, fraud protection, spam text protection.
3. Problem exact match: suspicious text, package text scam, bank impersonation, QR scam.
4. Discovery campaign with Search Match and broad terms; move winners into exact campaigns and add negatives.

Use distinct custom product pages for self-protection and family-support intent. Apple allows different screenshots, preview, promotional text, deep link, and URL per page. [Apple ad variations](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/best-practices/ad-variations)

### Google — second

Start with high-intent Search around scam verification and recovery. Add YouTube/Performance Max only after there is enough conversion and retained-cohort data to prevent optimization toward low-quality installs.

Upload native horizontal, vertical, and later square assets. Google recommends at least one 10–60 second vertical asset for Shorts eligibility and multiple orientations for wider campaign coverage. [Google Performance Max video requirements](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14528532)

### Meta — creative learning, not immediate scale

Use Reels and Stories to test hooks and audiences. Meta recommends 9:16 for full-screen placements, faster motion-led storytelling, and key elements inside safe zones. [Meta aspect-ratio guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/103816146375741), [Meta Stories creative guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/304846896685564)

Start with two audiences:

- adults 45+ interested in financial safety, consumer protection, and caregiving;
- adult children/caregivers who want a respectful way to support a parent or relative.

Do not use a vulnerable-person list, uploaded private evidence, or fear-based retargeting. Exclude existing activated users from acquisition campaigns.

## Budget gates

### Validation: $2,500–$7,500 total

- 50% Apple Ads;
- 30% Google Search;
- 20% Meta creative tests.

Run small, stable cells long enough to observe activation. Pause any cell that attracts installs without first checks.

### Repeatability: $10,000–$30,000/month

Release only after:

- App Store page and privacy disclosures are final;
- attribution and privacy-minimized product events are validated;
- activation is at least 60%;
- median time to first check is under two minutes;
- D30 retention among activated users is at least 25%;
- support can answer within one business day;
- no unresolved critical/high security issue exists.

## Measurement

Primary conversion ladder:

1. ad impression;
2. App Store product-page view;
3. install;
4. onboarding complete;
5. first check complete;
6. safer next action viewed/completed;
7. second active week;
8. D30 retained.

Decision metrics:

- cost per first check;
- cost per protected decision;
- cost per activated D30 user;
- retained-user rate by hook, page, and channel;
- false reassurance and unsafe-advice incidents;
- support contacts per 1,000 acquired users;
- invite acceptance and referral coefficient.

Views, clicks, and installs are diagnostics, not the objective.

## Marketing beyond ads

The stronger scale engines for this category are credibility and distribution:

1. **Credit-union and regional-bank pilots:** sponsored access, member education, and privacy-preserving aggregate outcomes.
2. **Libraries, senior centers, caregiver groups, and employers:** workshops built around “pause and verify,” not a sales pitch.
3. **Consumer reporters and local media:** demonstrate a current scam pattern, exact limitations, and the official verification route.
4. **Official-source SEO:** deep pages for bank/government impersonation, parcel/toll, investment, romance, job, marketplace, QR, account takeover, and recovery.
5. **App Store optimization:** product-page tests and audience-specific custom pages before broad social spend.
6. **Trust assets:** methodology, privacy summary, security contact, update history, and eventually independently verified evaluation results.

## Claim rules

Approved direction:

- “Decision support for suspicious requests.”
- “See the pressure signals and a safer next step.”
- “Verify independently.”
- “Help without taking over.”
- “Decision support—not a guarantee that something is safe.”

Prohibited direction:

- “100% safe,” “guaranteed protection,” or “blocks every scam”;
- implying live cellular-call recording or universal call blocking;
- implying bank, police, government, or platform affiliation;
- promising to recover funds;
- manufactured testimonials, ratings, rescues, or user counts.
