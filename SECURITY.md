# Security policy

PauseSure welcomes responsible reports that help protect people using the website or app.

## Reporting

Email `security@pausesure.com` with:

- the affected URL, app version, or component;
- a concise description of the issue and likely impact;
- reproducible steps or a minimal proof of concept; and
- a safe way to contact you.

Do not include passwords, payment information, government identifiers, private scam evidence, or another person's data. If evidence contains personal information, redact it before sending.

## Safe research expectations

- Do not access, change, destroy, or retain data that is not yours.
- Do not perform denial-of-service, social-engineering, physical-security, or automated high-volume testing.
- Stop testing and report promptly if you encounter personal data, credentials, or an active security incident.
- Give PauseSure reasonable time to investigate and correct a validated issue before public disclosure.

## Scope and response

The current public website and the latest supported PauseSure app release are in scope. Third-party services and issues that require tricking a person are out of scope, but useful reports may still be redirected to the appropriate provider.

PauseSure will acknowledge credible reports when the mailbox is operational, assess severity, and coordinate remediation and disclosure. This policy is not a promise of payment and does not authorize activity that would be unlawful or harmful.
